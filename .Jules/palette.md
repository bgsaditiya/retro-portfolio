## 2024-05-18 - [Improved Form Feedback]
**Learning:** Added `aria-live` and `role="status"` to dynamically displayed elements like form submission success messages to make them accessible to screen readers, along with visually prominent visual markers for required fields. In addition, providing interactive feedback during submission delays is a key component to better UX.
**Action:** Always check form elements for clear required markings, focus rings, disabled states during submit, and accessible live regions for dynamic messages.
